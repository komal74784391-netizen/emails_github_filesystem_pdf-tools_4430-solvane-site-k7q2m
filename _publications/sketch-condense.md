Accepted at ICLR 2025.

We propose a learnable sketching operator that condenses large input graphs
into compact synthetic graphs while retaining the accuracy of graph neural
networks trained on the condensed data. Experiments on seven benchmarks
show consistent gains over fixed condensation heuristics.
